| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|

| **Observed** | Reported by the researcher or seen during the incident, with no preserved artifact |
| **Inferred** | Consistent with the evidence but not directly shown |
| **Unknown** | Cannot be determined from the available artifacts |

## Findings that could not be independently verified

1. **"Domain registered 2026-10-13."** Not supported. That is the TLS certificate's start date.
   urlscan reports the apex as about 10.6 years old. No WHOIS lookup was made.
2. **Linux triage results** (processes, sockets, units, timers, cron, autostart, journal). No
   command output was preserved.
3. **Attribution of historical Java processes.** One was a Gradle daemon. The others are
   unattributed.
4. **Browser configuration** (download prompt, no auto-open, wallet site access). Reported, not
   exported.
5. **Site permissions for the domain.** Reviewed, but the results were not preserved.
6. **Email authenticity and the sender's identity.** No headers were available, and the 09:02
   attachments were not analysed.
7. **Which `/api/join` branch ran.** Strongly suggested by the download record, but not recorded
   directly.
8. **Whether the operator varied responses by platform.** The server-side logic is unobservable.
9. **Chromium enum meanings** (`state=2`, `interrupt_reason=40`) come from Chromium source
   conventions, not from Brave's exact build.

## Limitations

- No memory acquisition, full-disk image, or AV/EDR scan was performed.
- The browser history and cache were cleared before the DIPS copy. Cache entries that might have
  held the `/api/join` response are gone.
- The filesystem timeline file is empty.
- urlscan captured the site at 09:07, after the visits. The server could have behaved differently
  for the target at 09:00 or for another code.
- The static analysis covers the two archived scripts only. The HTML document
  (`0f4fd4d9...a436`) and CSS were not examined in detail.
